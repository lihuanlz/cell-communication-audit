# Scale-free statistics are the currency of cellular communication: an audit across four signalling systems
Huan Li^1^

^1^Shanghai Liangta Biotech Co., Ltd., Shanghai 201899, P. R. China

Corresponding author: HL@liangtabio.com (H.L)


## Abstract

Cells compute with biochemical circuits whose component concentrations vary from cell to cell and cannot be calibrated against any shared reference. We recently showed, on the assay side, that under such multiplicative scale uncertainty only scale-free statistics (counts, ratios, event times) survive transmission. Here we ask the natural follow-up: is this how cells themselves communicate? We answer by auditing four signalling systems with one discipline: derive the degeneracy group of each model, identify the statistics invariant under it, and test where those statistics live in data. A static covalent-modification sensor is structurally blind to all absolute molecular scales (four-dimensional degeneracy group; Fisher conditioning ${\sim}30$ for shape versus ${\sim}10^{300}$ for scale). The p53 pulse encoder places its parameter information overwhelmingly on timing rather than amplitude channels (a 94.2% versus 5.5% decomposition of the representative model under the stated calibration; SI S13.3), and its dose response is a counting law. In bacterial chemotaxis, the amplitude description of the receptor layer is internally inconsistent in the best public single-cell data, while the scale-free Weber relation is exact, and blind predictions on the two coordinates succeed and fail accordingly. In GPCR pharmacology, 21.5% of the field's archived potency parameters fail the model's own identities. Seven pre-registered blind adjudications on six independent single-cell datasets bound the framework empirically, falsifications recorded alongside hits. Reading the same principle across signalling cascades shows the stratification propagating with it: amplitude fractures are confined to the across-cell gain distribution, and the single actively maintained reference sits at the relay that must absorb the upstream fracture. Across four systems, absolute-scale descriptions are unidentifiable, inconsistent, or unused; scale-free statistics are identifiable, consistent, and information-bearing. Cells communicate in scale-free statistics because the scale channel is uncalibratable.

## Introduction

A cell that reports a number to itself or to its neighbours faces a measurement problem no instrument engineer would accept. The components of its signalling circuits (kinases, receptors, transcription factors) are present in counts that vary by tens of per cent from cell to cell and drift over hours; no two cells share a calibration standard, and no external reference exists against which an intracellular concentration can be read out absolutely. Yet cells routinely transmit dose, identity and urgency with quantitative precision. How?

We recently formalised the abstract version of this problem for bioanalytical assays. In the Target-Capture Scaling (TCS) framework, a measurement whose observable is a multiplicative function of uncalibratable scale factors possesses a degeneracy group: the family of parameter transformations that leave every observable invariant. What survives the group, and is therefore reportable without calibration, are scale-free statistics: counts, ratios, ordinals and event times. Assay platforms that report absolute analogue scale must pay for calibration; platforms that count (digital PCR being the limiting case) are calibration-free by construction. The companion preprint^1^ establishes this taxonomy and its payment theorem on the assay side.

The present paper transfers the question from assays to cells: **if the intracellular environment is uncalibratable in absolute scale, what statistics can cellular communication actually use, and do real signalling systems in fact use them?** We answer with an audit, not an argument. The audit is a fixed five-step discipline, applied identically to every system: write the input–output relation in dimensionless form; derive the degeneracy group exactly; stratify parameter directions by Fisher information into identifiable, sloppy and degenerate tiers; replace point estimates with one-sided bounds where likelihoods plateau; and name the orthogonal measurement that would break each degeneracy. The audit returns, for each system, a stratification of what any experiment on its standard observables can in principle return, and therefore which statistics are candidates for carrying information through an uncalibratable interior.

We apply the audit to four systems that together span the organisational levels of cellular signalling: a static covalent-modification sensor (the phosphorylation–dephosphorylation cycle, PdPC), a dynamic pulse encoder (the p53 DNA-damage response), a chemosensory receptor layer (bacterial chemotaxis, in the best public single-cell dose–response data), and a pharmacological meta-level (GPCR concentration–response parameters archived across a field database). Two unifying layers complete the programme: a theorem-level analysis showing that the two known routes to calibration-freeness (chemical saturation and circuit topology) collapse the likelihood onto counting statistics in the same way (the digital limit); and a set of seven pre-registered blind adjudications on six independent public single-cell datasets that test the framework's extrapolations and record its falsifications verbatim. All audits were executed under one bookkeeping discipline (pre-registration, frozen verdict cards, and double-recording of successes and failures) described in Methods.

The result is a single pattern with four independent instances: in every system, the absolute-scale description is either structurally invisible, internally inconsistent, or demonstrably unused, while scale-free statistics (counts, event times, ratios, midpoints) are identifiable, internally consistent, and carry the information. Cellular communication, like assay engineering, runs on what cannot be miscalibrated.

## Results

### The framework: degeneracy groups, surviving statistics, and the price of absolute scale

All four audits below are instances of one mathematical object, which we state once here (full formalism in SI S1). Model a signalling pathway as a cascade of layers $L_1 \to \dots \to L_n$, where layer $i$ with parameters $\theta_i$ maps its upstream input to an output symbol $Y_i$, with biochemical noise absorbed into the layer's conditional distribution. Each layer carries a **degeneracy group** $G_i$: the set of parameter transformations that leave the output distribution invariant for every input. What any downstream measurement can in principle learn about the layer is exactly the content invariant under $G_i$: the group's orbit invariants; everything else is gauge. A layer-boundary rule makes this operational: parameters that are indistinguishable from the same output belong to the same layer, so information structure, not biochemical topology, defines the layers (feedback included).

Within a layer, an input statistic $X$ (a fold-change, a sign, a count, an event time, an amplitude) either survives or leaks. Formally, $X$ is in the layer's **survival set** if the distribution of the transmitted alphabet $a_i(Y_i)$ it induces is invariant under the group action up to the noise floor (total-variation distance below a noise-calibrated $\varepsilon$ over the physiological working range). The survival set is computable, and for the generic case of an unidentified affine gain it has a sharp algebraic content: the unconditional survivors are difference ratios (fold-changes with zero baseline), signs, counts and event times; amplitude survives only if the gain is pinned from outside; increments survive only where ratios survive and the gain is pinned to the precision the downstream consumer requires. A statistic crosses an interface if and only if three legs hold simultaneously: algebraic survival ($X$ in the survival set), symbolic reachability ($I(X; Y_i) > 0$), and temporal bandwidth (the statistic's timescale lies inside the interface's measured perturbation–response bandwidth). Cascades compose by an exact information-loss identity: writing $L_i = I(S; Y_{i-1}) - I(S; Y_i)$ for the loss at layer $i$ of a Markov chain $S \to Y_1 \to \dots \to Y_n$, one has $H(S) = I(S; Y_n) + \sum_i L_i$ with monotone mutual information, so the end-to-end transferable content of any statistic is limited by the weakest layer, the layer with the largest loss, which the identity locates without any capacity concept. The framework's status is stated precisely: the information-loss identity and monotonicity are theorems given the model; the survival set and three-leg criterion are operational definitions; no tightness or capacity claim is made.

Payment enters through references. Quantities a pathway can use fall into three classes: class I, calibration-free survivors (ratios, signs, counts, event times; no reference needed); class II, structural constants set by evolution and maintained by physics ($K_d$, $K_m$: dimensional converters that trigger no signature); and class III, paid references (set points, internal standards; actively maintained by the cell at continuing energetic cost, diagnosed by operational markers: negative feedback acting on the reference itself, recovery of the reference after direct perturbation, drift of the reference when energy supply is inhibited). The **payment theorem** inherited from the assay side then reads: wherever a function references absolute scale, a class-III reference must be established and maintained at that position; the positions are determined by functional demand, not by where information is lost. Three prospective falsifiable predictions follow (SI S1.6): deleting a class-III maintenance mechanism must abolish absolute-scale sensitivity while sparing relative-scale sensitivity (P-1); a synthetic circuit with no active internal standard must fail to transmit stable absolute scale (P-2); and positions that functionally require absolute position but show no reported maintenance machinery constitute an exhaustive search list whose hits, or exhaustively documented absence, adjudicate the framework (P-3).

The four system audits below instantiate the framework at four organisational levels. In each case the audit (i) derives the degeneracy group exactly, (ii) computes the survival set or its Fisher-information image, and (iii) checks which class the system's working statistics belong to, and in two cases goes further, testing prospectively whether the framework's predictions survive contact with data it has never seen.

### The static sensor is blind to its own absolute scale

The phosphorylation–dephosphorylation cycle, the covalent-modification motif at the heart of kinase signalling, converts an input activity ratio into a steady-state modified fraction. With six physical parameters (two catalytic rates, the Michaelis constants, two enzyme totals, substrate total), its steady-state input–output curve is the standard object fitted to dose–response data. The audit's first step is a demonstration: four parameter sets differing in every individual parameter by up to five-fold produce steady-state curves that are not similar but identical to the last digit of double-precision arithmetic (max $|\Delta u| = 0$ over the full titration range). No experiment on this curve, at any precision, distinguishes them. What the curve can return is not six parameters but exactly what the four sets share: two dimensionless combinations: the activity ratio $\xi$ and the saturation ratio $\kappa = K_m/S_T$.

This is Theorem 1 of the audit: the steady-state curve depends on the six physical parameters only through these two groups; the degeneracy group is four-dimensional, and all absolute molecular scales are structurally unidentifiable from steady-state fitting. The asymmetric-Michaelis extension raises the identifiable set to the triple $(\xi, \kappa_1, \kappa_2)$ with the closed-form local slope $n_{\mathrm{H}} = 4/(4 - 1/(\kappa_1+\tfrac{1}{2}) - 1/(\kappa_2+\tfrac{1}{2}))$, and leaves the group four-dimensional; embedding the cycle in a positive-feedback switch (critical gain $g^{*} = 0.443$ at $\kappa = 0.05$) moves the operating point but not the group; the same four actions leave every branch of the bistable response invariant to machine precision. Theorem 2 quantifies the barrier at realistic noise: the shape tier ($\ln c$, $\ln \kappa$) is well conditioned (condition number $\chi \approx 30$) while the absolute-scale block carries an exact zero eigenvalue (effective $\chi \sim 10^{300}$; the quoted value is the deep-saturation extrapolation, with the archived code returning up to $\chi \sim 10^{33}$ at the deepest $\kappa$ values in double precision). No increase in sample size or precision crosses this barrier; only an orthogonal measurement axis, substrate or enzyme titration, does. Theorem 3 supplies the reporting protocol for the one informative boundary: the local Hill slope obeys the closed form $n_{\mathrm{H}} = 1 + 1/(2\kappa)$, so $\kappa$ is recoverable from curve shape, but below $\kappa \lesssim 0.01$ the confidence width diverges and the honest statement is a scan-based one-sided bound $\kappa \leq \kappa_{\max}$, never a spurious point estimate.

The audit also locates where the cycle should sit on its own $\kappa$-axis if it serves as a sensor. Steady-state mutual information between a log-normal input ensemble and the noisy output has an interior optimum ($\kappa^{*} \approx 0.05$–$0.1$ for narrow input distributions, 2.85 bits; $\kappa^{*} \approx 1$–$3$ for wide ones, 3.04 bits), while the celebrated zero-order ultrasensitive limit^2^ is information-poor for all input widths (1.1–1.4 bits): per snapshot, the infinite-gain switch is a decision element, not a measurement element. The static sensor's contribution to the main line is thus already double-edged: its absolute scale is invisible in principle, and its information-optimal operating point is an interior, scale-relative coordinate, not an absolute one.

### The dynamic encoder carries its information in time, not amplitude

The p53 tumour suppressor responds to DNA double-strand breaks with discrete, stereotyped pulses whose amplitude, duration and first-pulse timing are approximately dose-independent while their number increases with dose.^3–6^ The audit of this encoder proceeds at three levels: what the pulse observables imply about circuit topology, what they can identify at all, and what the published single-cell record confirms.

**Pulse observables select topology.** Partitioning pulse-generator circuits into a negative-feedback-only class (NF) and an excitable class (EXC, containing a positive loop), three output-side signatures discriminate the classes: onset continuity of the amplitude–dose relation, hysteresis width under up- versus down-scans, and the range sensitivity $\alpha = \partial\ln A/\partial\ln D$. By the Thomas–Soulé theorem^7^, an NF circuit's generic destabilisation is a Hopf onset with $A(D) \propto \sqrt{D - D_c}$; every NF circuit possesses a dose range of graded, strongly dose-dependent amplitude (Lemma 1; continuation-grade numerics: $A^2 \propto (s - s_H)$ at $R^2 = 0.9926$, with the subcritical escape hatch bounded separately; measured hysteresis width 0.275 against a normal-form prediction of 0.25). Excitable onset is all-or-none: amplitude is set by fast-subsystem branch geometry and is independent of drive exactly in the singular limit (Lemma 2; 6.5% variation over a 4.6-fold drive range; a Morris–Lecar Type-II control gives $\alpha = -0.258$). The joint three-signature triple uniquely assigns universality class at experimental (~15%) amplitude precision (Theorem 4). Published p53 statistics, dose-independent amplitude ($|\alpha| \lesssim 0.03$) with dose-counting pulse number across the 100-fold $\gamma$-irradiation range, place the system deep in the excitable region, excluding pure negative-feedback models as sufficient architectures and converting a two-decade modelling intuition into a signature-based exclusion. A built-in control exists within a single study: the same protein in the same cell line shows excitable dose-counting pulses after double-strand breaks but a graded, non-excitable single pulse after UV^8^. Source-digitised, the amplitude–dose slopes are $\alpha_{\mathrm{NCS}} = -0.001 \pm 0.113$ versus $\alpha_{\mathrm{UV}} = +0.981 \pm 0.114$ (bootstrap $\times 20000$), 6.1 combined standard deviations apart under the usual independent-error combination (the frozen adjudication cards quote the separation as 8.6 under their pre-registered one-sided-denominator convention; both conventions are documented in SI S3.3), with the counting-law slope inverting to the damage-repair timescale $\tau_r \approx 7.7$ h on the digitised scale (SI S13.4).

**The channel census.** Propagating the Fisher information of six pulse statistics onto the seven dynamical parameters of the excitable representative yields the information-theoretic quantification of the topology result: under the stated noise calibration, 94.2% of the total parameter information rides on timing channels (pulse width 60.2%, count 18.2%, period 11.5%, first-pulse time 4.4%), leaving 5.5% on amplitude. Within this representative, amplitude is not merely dose-invariant; it carries almost no information about any dynamical parameter. A mechanism-level recheck on the published 23-parameter p53 circuit^9^ preserves the ordering, timing dominant and amplitude near-empty, while the numerical share moves with the noise calibration (45.1% nominal; SI S13.3): the decomposition is a calibrated statement, not a structural invariant. And population-level gain heterogeneity kills the residual amplitude channel while leaving timing intact: as per-cell gain dispersion grows to 0.6, $\mathrm{CV}(A)$ rises $0.002 \to 0.639$ while $\mathrm{CV}(T)$ stays pinned at $0.016$ in the representative ensemble. This is the two-layer death of the amplitude channel: geometric at the single-cell layer, statistical at the population layer. Audit step 3 closes the loop: the exact zero direction of the Fisher spectrum equals the degeneracy-group orbit tangent, and the structural parameters attain the Cramér–Rao bound: the three predictions of the conditional digital-limit theorem are verified numerically on this circuit.

**Noise is a null direction; its signature is not.** The noise amplitude $\sigma$ is an exact zero direction of the first-moment pulse statistics: Fisher eigenvalue ratio $\lambda_7/\lambda_1 \leq 4.7 \times 10^{-19}$, at the floor of double precision and stable across three numerical configurations. Information about the fluctuation-generating noise lives exclusively in second-moment, dispersion-type observables. Two dispersion signatures then do topology work. First, the ratio $\mathrm{Var}(t_1)/\mathrm{Var}(T)$ is bounded at $O(1)$ (0.03–0.12) for any bare oscillator, physically incapable of producing the literature value 5.8, while an independent stochastic sensing delay upstream of the oscillator core drives the ratio across the threshold monotonically and reproduces $5.7 \approx 5.8$ at delay dispersion $\mu_d \approx T$: the $t_1$/IPI dispersion ratio is a sufficient signature of an independent stochastic sensing stage, and its magnitude inverts the upstream delay dispersion. Second, the (population $\mathrm{CV}(T)$, intra-cell $\mathrm{IPI}_{\mathrm{CV}}$) plane separates fluctuation sources by time colour: slow parameter heterogeneity inflates only CV(T) (intra-cell $\mathrm{IPI}_{\mathrm{CV}}$ pinned at 0.083), while fast white noise moves both axes; the measured $\mathrm{IPI}_{\mathrm{CV}} = 0.30$ can only arise from fast intra-cellular stochasticity.

**The counting channel is the working channel.** The mutual information between dose and pulse count is $I(D;N) = 3.07$ of 3.17 bits (96.8%) over a four-fold dose range, near-lossless transmission through the one channel the degeneracy group cannot touch. The encoder's design logic, read off the audit, is the main line's second instance: dose information is placed in an alphabet (counts, intervals, timing)^10,11^ that is invariant under the multiplicative scale group of the intracellular environment, because the amplitude alphabet is both unidentifiable and information-free. The molecular layer closes underneath the observables: in the reconstructed 23-parameter circuit, the period is set by the dominant Jacobian eigenvalue (5.95 h against the simulated 5.48 h), the interpulse interval, first-pulse delay and pulse width each close against their measured bands, and the pulse width decomposes into a plateau segment plus an Mdm2-recovery-dominated collapse tail (SI S14). An independent closed-form check against a logistic-substitution delay model of the core reproduces the measured period within 2% (measured 5.5 h, closed form 5.60 h, simulation 5.48 h), once its parameters are read as stressed-state quantities. The period-from-constants direction has prior art in simplified cores^12–14^; the six-species phase-share attribution and its audit trail are contributed here.

### Two roads to one digital limit

The static audit and the dynamic audit each isolate a regime in which measurement becomes calibration-free, reached by opposite means: in the covalent cycle, by chemical saturation ($\kappa \to 0$); in the pulse encoder, by circuit topology ($\alpha \to 0$). We prove constructively for these two circuits that when the analogue scale factor cancels from the likelihood, the inferable information degenerates to counting statistics. Geometrically, in both limits the Fisher matrix retains a single nonzero eigenvalue whose eigenvector lies along the partition-counting direction, while curvature along the absolute-scale direction vanishes; the residual stochasticity is combinatorial (binomial/Poisson partition counts; pulse counts with jittered timing), not parametric; and only dimensionless ratios survive (the kinase/phosphatase activity ratio in one case, $D_0/D_c$ and $\tau_r/T$ in the other). We call this shared structure the digital limit. The conditional theorem proved for the two circuits has an unconditional generalisation (a stratified classification theorem over degeneracy groups, a universal functional theorem, and a noise-robustness bound) given in SI S5.

Within its stated domain (single channel, single readout, memoryless, passive sensing) counting is the unique calibration-free channel that costs no extra resource. The domain matters: fold-change, Weber-law and ratiometric schemes are established calibration-free analogue strategies^15^, but all pay extra resources (temporal memory, multi-point sampling, or a second channel) and all cancel mainly the common-mode gain direction; a fold-change of counts is still counting. The perspective reorganises two celebrated results. The zero-order ultrasensitive switch sits at the digital limit of the static channel and is therefore information-poor as a sensor ($\approx 1$ bit per snapshot): a decision element, reporting threshold crossing with maximal reliability precisely because its output is scale-free. The p53 pulse train sits at the digital limit of the dynamic channel: a measurement element that encodes dose in counts with logarithmic range, robust because it never exposes an analogue scale. Between the two limits lies the analogue regime: information-rich (the interior optimum $\kappa^{*}$) but calibration-hungry, usable only where the degenerate scales are pinned by orthogonal measurements or tight regulation. We state the resulting conjecture precisely enough to be attacked: if the likelihood-relevant partition statistics of a channel's output are invariant under the degeneracy group of the intracellular environment, those statistics must be of counting type. Its non-tautological content, known non-counterexamples (the coefficient of variation; continuous ratio statistics), and its boundary against the representational theory of measurement are delimited in SI S5.

Going digital has costs: an excitable system must be maintained near-critical at continuous energy expense, and counting at low copy numbers is Poisson-limited. Analogue grading is more economical in the shallow regime (few resolvable levels), counting wins in the deep regime, with a crossover at roughly 3–4 resolvable levels. This trade-off rationalises the observed distribution of regimes: p53, facing catastrophic events that demand high-fidelity decisions, counts; metabolic sensing that requires continuous fine-tuning stays analogue.

### Blind adjudications bound the claim empirically

A framework that explains everything explains nothing, so we pre-registered its extrapolations and let public data rule on them. Seven blind adjudications (P2–P7, plus one archived framework self-correction) were executed on six independent published single-cell datasets, each with decision lines frozen before unblinding, scripts with fixed seeds, and verdict cards archived verbatim, hits and falsifications alike. Two adjudications (P6, P7) asked the framework's sharpest question directly, fold-change versus absolute-scale attribution, on sequential-stimulus data. The ledger is part of the result.

Two clauses are clean hits. On the Msn2 nine-promoter $\times$ four-dose raw trajectories^16^, the calibration-free event-time channel was completely insensitive to cell-size stratification ($|\Delta| \leq 0.01$, reaching the pre-registered line in six of seven promoters at $k = 3$, with 7/7 in the $k = 2$ arm and 6/7 in the $k = 4$ arm), a first blind hit for the survivor class. On the NF-κB microfluidic spatial-gradient trajectories^17^, per-cell self-referenced free statistics (first-crossing event time against the cell's own pre-stimulus baseline, with all cross-cell calibration explicitly forbidden) decoded spatial ordinality across distance terciles at AUC = 0.907 and 0.819, both far from the frozen line.

Six clauses are falsifications, and they do the bounding work. On the same Msn2 data, the prediction that size heterogeneity must be priced into the molecule-number channel was ruled wrong in all 21/21 units (stratification penalty $\Delta \in [-0.049, +0.029]$; bootstrap CIs centred on zero, 19 of 21 crossing it): the cell-side redemption of invariance pricing does not run through the size-gain axis. On the NF-κB gradient data, the stimulus-duration axis failed outright (AUC 0.591/0.521 against the 0.60 line) and the high-dose ordinal axis failed on saturation (0.518 at $30{\leftrightarrow}100$ ng/ml, where the paid peak-amplitude statistic still decodes at 0.761). The ERK re-adjudication on GPCR-KTR dose series^18^ (7,393 and 7,508 cells across six doses, two ligands) falsified free event-time dose decoding (all eight AUCs below the 0.60 line) and the stratification-invariance clause ($|\Delta| \geq 0.03$ in 5/10 units). The boundary-predictor test on GPCR→Ca²⁺ single-cell data^19^ (195 valid cells) falsified the dead-zone clause (2/4 units out of the [0.40, 0.60) band) and returned only intermediate counting-channel decoding, while the dataset's population-level response rate and mean count rise monotonically across the full 100-fold dose range ($0.015 \to 0.918$; $0.02 \to 4.36$ counts). The clause contracts accordingly: the sufficiency of the free statistics is bounded by unsaturated ordinal axes and by system morphology; population-level counting can be intact where per-cell decoding is weak.

Two further adjudications (P6, P7) targeted the framework's sharpest dividing question, does a pathway read fold-change or absolute scale, on sequential-stimulus designs, and both returned power-limited intermediates that we report rather than resolve post hoc. On NF-κB sequential-stimulation trajectories^20^ (18 valid units), the fold-change-versus-absolute attribution was undecidable at the available power ($\Delta R^2 = -0.0014$, bootstrap CI $[-0.252, 0.153]$ crossing zero): verdict intermediate, with the power limitation recorded on the card. On the chemotaxis background ladder itself^21^ (25 condition units in the first pass, 30 in the erratum re-adjudication), the same attribution on the amplitude coordinate was likewise intermediate ($\Delta R^2 = +0.118$ in the first pass and $-0.091$ in the erratum re-adjudication under the corrected unit definition, both CIs crossing zero): the amplitude table does not adjudicate its own coordinate system, which is precisely the ambiguity the receptor-layer rupture below makes structural. Across the full ledger (seven adjudications, fourteen pre-registered clauses) the count stands at two clean hits, five intermediates, six falsifications and one dataset-level limitation; every clause, frozen line and unit value is in SI S7.

One earlier falsification is on the record by design. A previous version of this programme predicted, by mechanistic extrapolation from the static gain grading of the PdPC, that ERK pulse amplitude should grade with dose; source-figure discrimination on public data returned $\alpha \approx 0$ and falsified it. In the full version history, the discrimination theorem's classifications on data have never been wrong; the one failure was a mechanistic extrapolation across readout levels, and the record is kept because the falsifiability of the framework is a fact, not a rhetorical posture. Two adjudications of a different kind are archived alongside: a proposed mechanistic patch for the NF-κB model–experiment gap (switch-ified IκBα degradation) was computationally audited and ruled wrong (parameter-unreachability test), and the saturated-transient hypothesis for the same gap was quantitatively rejected. The framework adjudicates hypotheses about systems, not only classifications of systems.

### The analogue account ruptures at the receptor layer

The third instance is a stress test that failed, the framework's most instructive result. Bacterial chemotaxis is the canonical precision-sensing system^22^, and its best public single-cell dataset (ref. 21: FRET dose–responses across seven background levels, five foreground steps each, 57–268 cells per background level) admits two statistics that must, under the standard MWC-plus-perfect-adaptation model^23–25^, be generated by one parameter set: the amplitude table $R(B, F)$ (population medians) and the per-cell midpoint distribution $K_{1/2}(B)$. They cannot be. Along the full Pareto front of joint fits, no point achieves amplitude $R^2 > 0.9$ together with midpoint error $< 0.1$ dex: amplitude-favouring fits require cooperativity $N \approx 12$–$39$ and a non-physical saturation $a_{\max} = 1.4$–$2.7$; midpoint-favouring fits require $N \approx 2$–$4$ and destroy the amplitude table. Eight minimal repair paths (two subpopulations, imperfect adaptation, protocol mismatch, readout nonlinearity, ligand depletion, background-dependent gain, heterogeneous censored populations, asymmetric cooperativity) were excluded one by one under a uniform acceptance criterion, with all failures double-recorded including three of our own pipeline errors caught and repaired mid-course. The rupture is not a population-sampling artefact: rebuilding both statistics from strictly the same estimable cells leaves the Pareto front unmoved. It is a statement about the standard model class itself.

What survives the rupture is the point. The measured per-cell midpoints obey a textbook Weber line, $K_{1/2} = 1.17 \cdot (1.95 + B)$ µM, with maximum deviation 0.10 dex across seven backgrounds spanning four orders of magnitude of ligand (0.01–100 µM), a pure ratio law, invariant under any rescaling of absolute sensitivity. The scale-free statistic is exact; the absolute-scale parameterisation is inconsistent. The receptor layer, the place where the analogue description should be strongest, is where the audit finds it broken, and finds the surviving, exact description to be the scale-free one.

The dataset's mixed-ligand arm then provided something rarer than a retrospective fit: a prospective test. From the rupture analysis we derived blind predictions for two unseen mixed-ligand arms (one MeAsp-foreground arm on a mixed MeAsp + L-Asp background, one L-Asp-foreground arm on a MeAsp-dominated background) froze them with tolerances before touching the corresponding data, and scored them per clause (SI S8.6). Predictions made on the scale-free coordinate (the per-cell midpoint on the total effective ligand axis) hit in every scored reading (V1, V2, and the $\mathrm{V2}_{\mathrm{alt}}$ ambiguity control: deviations +0.014, +0.038 and +0.031 dex against a frozen $\pm 0.15$ dex tolerance; the point prediction for the foreground-axis midpoint read 0.86 µM against measured 1.24–1.34 µM). Predictions made on the absolute-amplitude coordinate missed in both primary arms (maximum deviations 0.105 and 0.126 against a frozen 0.08 tolerance in the response-amplitude clauses; the amplitude-coordinate structural test likewise missed). Blind prediction succeeds exactly on the statistics the framework says should survive, and fails exactly on the coordinate the audit found broken. For the main line this is the negative-image instance with a prospective edge: not only does the absolute-scale account of sensing fail on its own terms; its failures and its survivors are both predictable in advance, from the coordinate system alone.

### The field's analogue currency fails its own audit

The fourth instance steps one level up, from single systems to the quantitative currency in which an entire field reports signalling. GPCR pharmacology reports ligand action in absolute-scale parameters, affinity (p$K_{\mathrm{i}}$/p$K_{\mathrm{A}}$), potency (p$\mathrm{EC}_{50}$), and derived bias factors ($\Delta\log(\tau/K_A)$), and its operational model (Black–Leff)^26,27^ supplies fit-free algebraic identities among them, so that internal consistency can be checked directly. We audited three anchor studies and the field database. In the D2R dataset, the functionally inferred affinity of the high-affinity partial agonists departs from their binding affinity by up to $\Delta = -3.5$ dex (21–23$\sigma$; bifeprunox), with the fracture located precisely at the ligands on which the study's "significant bias" reports load-bear; the non-equilibrium drift component of the effect is quantitatively explained by the authors' own measured binding kinetics (zero free parameters), while the absolute discrepancy survives. In AT1R, the same ligand's functional affinity differs between the Gq and $\beta$-arrestin arms by $\Delta = -2.05$ dex (5.0$\sigma$; replicated in a mutant), a cross-arm fracture with binding affinity sitting exactly between the two readings. In the μOR study, one assay column (GIRK) is systematically fractured across all ligands (0.68–0.97 dex, 3.9–8.4$\sigma$, located uniquely by two independent identities), the column on which the paper's therapeutic-window conclusion rests. At the meta level, the Biased Signaling Atlas's own arithmetic pipeline is exact (3,931/3,931 identity closures), but 21.5% of its 17,987 archived ligand pairs violate the model's cell-level identity by more than 0.3 dex (4.2% by more than 1 dex), and 15 of 66 multi-ligand papers (23%) exceed 0.3 dex median deviation; the database's inclusion choices silently dropped the fractured column of the μOR study, changing the evidence base of its conclusion.

We are explicit about what this instance does and does not claim. It does not claim that biased signalling is unreal; it does not adjudicate which side of any individual discrepancy is correct. It claims that the absolute-scale parameters in which the field reports communication strength fail internal consistency checks at a substantial rate, concentrated at exactly the load-bearing positions of quantitative conclusions, while the audit's own consistency checks, which are identity-based and therefore scale-free, are exact wherever they apply. Three robustness and repair layers back the claim (SI S9). The audit method's own false-positive rate was measured on synthetic null batteries before any verdict was issued (code 72); the headline rates survive common-mode error calibration, group-level aggregation and column-level jackknife (code 73); and the audit extends from diagnosis to repair: a constraint projection maps each fractured record to its nearest identity-consistent point with an attached fracture probability (code 77), and for the μOR column a minimal coordinate-aware extension of the operational model, admitting one additional arm coordinate, resolves the fracture outright (model comparison $\Delta\log\mathrm{ML} = +128.2$; residual median $0.811 \to 0.107$ dex; code 80). The fractures are therefore structural, not noise, and repairable at a price the model itself names. The currency fails the audit; the audit's currency does not.

### Carrier translation across three audited cascades

The four audits above treat each system at a single level. We next asked what happens to a carrier when signalling is read across a cascade, exploiting two datasets that each span two signalling levels at once (SI S17, S17b). In the M3R–ACh to Ca²⁺ cascade (353 single cells, seven doses, five repeated stimulations per cell)^19^, the receptor-layer fracture propagates to the population dose–response: per-experiment Hill fits of the Ca²⁺ peak amplitude yield EC$_{50}$ values scattered over a 32-fold range (median 0.63 µM, CV 1.16 across 26 experiments). Yet the same data show that the fracture is confined to the across-cell gain distribution: within-cell repeatability across the five identical stimulations is 3.4–3.7% at high dose, an order of magnitude tighter than the across-cell dispersion (CV 28–36%), and per-cell response ranks are preserved across adjacent doses (Spearman $\rho = 0.85$–$0.90$). A cell therefore cannot read its own absolute amplitude, because it does not know where it sits in the gain distribution; what remains reportable downstream is self-referenced statistics and, at the low-dose end, the digital event of responding at all (responding fraction falls from 1.00 to 0.24 across the 100-fold ladder). In the p53 cascade the same principle appears in complementary form: amplitude is discarded already at the sensing stage (the noise amplitude is an exact Fisher null direction, and pulse amplitude varies by 0.9% over a 12-fold drive range), and dose reaches the nucleus exclusively as event timing and pulse count. A third instance closes the triangle: in the bacterial chemotaxis chain, where the receptor-cluster layer carries the parameter rupture documented above, we verified in the FRET archive itself (2,217 cells, 77,595 pulse events) that the adaptation relay restores the pre-stimulus baseline to within 2.2% of the response amplitude after a 5 s washout, with no detectable dependence on the previous dose across a 90-fold range (SI S17b); the system's single paid reference, the methylation-maintained operating point, sits exactly at the middle stage that must absorb the upstream rupture before the digital motor switch downstream. Three cascades, three molecular hardwares, one rule: absolute amplitude does not survive the first stage, and where a paid reference exists, it is positioned at the relay that has to absorb the fracture.

Relation to prior art. Robust perfect adaptation as a design principle was established for bacterial chemotaxis^23^, fold-change detection as a mechanism of scale-invariant sensing was demonstrated in gene regulation and Wnt signalling^15,71^, and cell-to-cell variability of GPCR dose–response parameters was quantified in the source dataset of the first cascade itself^19^. What the cascade analysis adds is the quantitative connection between these observations and the degeneracy framework: the across-experiment EC$_{50}$ scatter and the within-cell repeatability are shown to be two coordinates of one gain distribution rather than two separate phenomena; the fracture is shown to be confined to that distribution; and the single paid reference is shown to sit at the relay that must absorb it. The rule stated above is thus a constraint on where references have to sit in any cascade whose upstream layer is uncalibrated, not a redescription of scale invariance.

### One pattern, four systems, one ledger

Fig. 1 summarises the convergence. Four systems, four organisational levels, four independent pre-registered audit programmes, one stratification: absolute-scale quantities are unidentifiable (PdPC degeneracy group, p53 amplitude channel), internally inconsistent (chemotaxis amplitude table), or unreliable at the field level (GPCR affinity fractures), while scale-free statistics are identifiable ($\kappa$ and $n_{\mathrm{H}}$; the p53 timing channels), exact (the Weber line), prospectively predictive (the mixed-ligand midpoint hits), and information-bearing (96.8% counting-channel efficiency). The digital-limit analysis shows the two constructive instances are the same instance: saturation and topology are two roads to the cancellation of the analogue scale factor from the likelihood. The blind-adjudication ledger (two clean hits, five intermediates, six falsifications and one dataset-level limitation across fourteen pre-registered clauses in seven adjudications) marks the empirical boundary of the claim with the same discipline that produced it. The degeneracy group that makes an assay uncalibratable is, inside the cell, the same mathematical object that makes amplitude untransmittable, and the statistics that survive it are the same: counts, ratios, ordinals, event times.


### Hijacked, not interrupted: the encoding chain under oncogenic mutation

The audits above characterise healthy encoding. We finally asked what the framework says about its breakdown, testing a specific chain of linked predictions in cancer cells with pre-registered verdict lines at every link (P11 to P16; Extended Data Fig. 16; SI S19). The chain asserts that oncogenic lesions do not interrupt signalling but hijack its encoding economics: a mutation that prices itself into the cheap direction (the dimensionless, calibration-free channels) should leave measurable traces at each level of the cascade. The first link is a falsification that sharpened the claim. In twelve-cell-line digitised p53 records^76^, our frozen prediction was that slow repair yields more pulses at fixed dose; the data reversed it (Spearman $\rho = +0.857$, one-sided $p = 0.0068$, seven oscillatory lines). Repair-deficient lines do not count faster; they abandon counting altogether, and sustained ATM activation switches p53 from the digital pulse regime to an analogue sustained regime. The counting law survives only inside the oscillatory regime, whose boundary is set by repair efficiency: a registered contraction of the framework, and the observed regime switch is the first link of the chain, a language change rather than a silence.

The second link reads the switch downstream. Under matched total p53 exposure (time-series RNA-seq and proteomics, oscillatory versus rising p53 arms; our own audit of the archived TP53 protein integrals gives an arm ratio of 1.65, which we correct for)^78^, 20.4% of induced genes still differ more than twofold between the two dynamical regimes, and the direction is mechanistically interpretable: arrest and terminal-fate genes (CDKN1A, MDM2, TP53I3, BTG2) are preferentially amplified by the sustained regime (AUC-corrected excess up to $+2.6$ bits), while repair genes (DDB2, XPC) track the exposure integral with shape-insensitive output ($\le 0.5$ bit excess). Two decoder classes, counting-type and level-type, coexist in the same nucleus; a cell line whose repair failure forces the sustained regime systematically over-reads arrest commands. The third link concerns the Ras-to-ERK channel. In isogenic MEF lines with single-allele Ras/Raf lesions (single-cell EKAR3 trajectories, 6 ligands, 4 doses)^77^, the mutual information between ligand dose and single-cell ERK output falls from 0.43 bits in KRAS$^{\mathrm{WT}}$ to 0.09 in KRAS$^{\mathrm{Q61R}}$ and 0.01 in BRAF$^{\mathrm{V600E}}$ (paired bootstrap 95% CIs exclude zero; robust across five discretisations and a continuous KSG estimator), while the weakest allele KRAS$^{\mathrm{G12V}}$ is unaffected: channel compression grades with allele strength, and a prospective point prediction for the untested intermediate allele KRAS-G13D ($\Delta I \in [-0.25, -0.05]$ bits) is frozen in SI S19. The fourth link asks where resistance mutations walk. On the ABL kinase axis, clinically emerging resistance mutants sit closer to the catalytic zero direction than predicted-but-never-clinical mutants (median $|\Delta\log_{10}(k_{\mathrm{cat}}/K_M)|$ 0.237 versus 0.705, fold ratio 2.94, one-sided $p = 0.033$); on an independent EGFR saturation-mutagenesis axis^79^, author-validated resistance variants preserve signalling activity above the missense background (median ratio 2.2, $p = 0.002$), with a registered counterexample subset (L718X) that buys resistance at the price of activity, splitting the resistance manifold into an activity-preserving branch and a binding-disrupting branch that requires compensatory background. The outer boundary of the chain is empirical: at bulk-tissue resolution (TCGA RPPA, four cancer types, ${\sim}1{,}000$ tumours), the predicted decoupling of phospho-ERK from upstream receptor abundance is not observable, because the coupling is absent even in wild-type tumours ($|r| \le 0.18$ in all four); the single-cell channel compression does not project onto bulk RPPA, and the chain's extrapolation to patient tissue requires single-cell-resolved measurement. Cancer, in the audited record, is not a broken telephone line: it is a line speaking a cheaper language, with downstream decoders reading the old price list.


## Discussion

The thesis of this paper is narrow and falsifiable: cellular communication is implemented in statistics invariant under the multiplicative-scale degeneracy group of the intracellular environment, because nothing else is reportable across it. Four audits support it from four directions, two constructive (what can be identified), two destructive (what fails on its own terms), and a pre-registered adjudication ledger records where its extrapolations failed.

**What the audit adds.** The information-theoretic literature has established how much information flows through biochemical circuits and what architectures optimise the flow^28–30^; the degeneracy literature has documented that behaviour underdetermines parameters^31–33^. The audit addresses the complementary epistemic question, what the flow can reveal about the circuit, and turns "parameters are poorly constrained" into a computable object: exactly which combinations are constrained, and how well, with reporting protocols attached. To be explicit: nondimensionalisation, degeneracy groups, Fisher stratification, scan-based one-sided bounds and orthogonal experimental design each have decades of precedent; we claim no methodological novelty, but novelty of application and output: exact degeneracy groups with reporting protocols for two circuits, a formal exclusion from observable signatures to topology classes (upgrading "tried and failed" to "impossible in principle"), a precisely stated falsifiable digital-limit conjecture, and two field-level inconsistency audits whose failure modes were not previously quantified.

**Consistency across systems.** The signature–topology correspondence is consistent with established physiology beyond the p53 case. In Xenopus oocyte maturation, the MAPK cascade responds with Hill coefficient $\gtrsim 35$ and positive feedback^34,35^, a near-digital static switch ($\kappa \lesssim 0.015$ by the $n_{\mathrm{H}} = 1 + 1/(2\kappa)$ relation) used as a decision element; in mammalian single cells the same ERK pathway produces frequency-modulated fixed-amplitude pulses^36^, the population-graded response arising from the time integral of pulse frequency; the analogue/digital classification is a property of the operating regime and of the readout level (single-cell events versus population integration). The NF-κB core circuit is a multi-step negative-feedback loop without a positive loop^37,38^; by Lemma 1 it cannot occupy the digital-pure regime, and the data^39^ agree at a finer resolution: inter-peak intervals do not encode (~75–95 min, constant), first-peak amplitude grades only weakly ($\alpha = 0.151$, the leaky-digital band), first-peak area is constant, and oscillation count increases with dose ($\approx +0.6$ per decade): instantaneous frequency does not encode, but pulse count does. At the tissue level, radioresistant tissues show oscillatory p53 (digital, measurement mode) whereas radiosensitive tissues show sustained p53 (switch, decision mode): the encoding architecture matches physiology.

**The gauge principle.** Because observables depend only on dimensionless combinations, the functionally complete description of a circuit lives in dimensionless coordinates; absolute molecular scales are a redundant gauge. Three practices follow. Experimentally, perturbations and cross-system comparisons are meaningful only when stated on dimensionless groups: comparing absolute rate constants across cell types conflates gauge with physics. Evolutionarily, mutation walks in absolute coordinates while selection reads dimensionless ones; the degeneracy group is the neutral-drift space available to a circuit, which formalises how ion-channel degeneracy supports resilience^32,33^. Functionally, the principle rationalises why biological sensing is so often ratio-based (fold-change detection^15^, morphogen-gradient scaling by tissue size, the kinase/phosphatase activity ratio): a cell has no external calibration standard, so evolution can only have built its sensors on dimensionless quantities. Counting is the limiting case in which the observable is dimensionless by construction.

**The free-first principle.** The audit record supports a stronger, cost-effectiveness reading: wherever a calibration-free statistic suffices for the function, it is the one used, and payment for an actively maintained reference occurs exactly where function must reference absolute scale, nowhere else (SI S1.6b). Table 1 collects the per-system carriers. The pattern is visible in every audited system: p53 encodes dose in pulse count while pulse amplitude is dose-independent^8^ and functionally silent^10^; Msn2 abandons amplitude stratification and keeps frequency; ERK digitises; bacterial chemotaxis reads fold changes over two orders of magnitude of absolute ligand level^61^ while paying for exactly one reference, the methylation-maintained effective receptor affinity; Wnt/β-catenin signalling reads fold change over background, with a quantised plateau invariant to ±5% across 0–50 mM LiCl^71^; and in the Drosophila embryo, the Bcd profile's length constant is an absolute anchor supplied free by physics (a pre-registered per-embryo test on 581 profiles: scaling exponent $\alpha = -0.18$, 95% CI $[-0.48, 0.11]$, excluding perfect scaling at ${\sim}7\sigma$), while gap-gene boundaries track relative position $x/L$ with degradation following nuclear density, a geometric counter that comes free with development^70,72,73^. A single counterexample, a paid reference maintained at a position whose function needs only relative quantities, falsifies the principle.

**Reverse predictions.** The cellular line of this programme has a machine-side sibling: artificial perception networks evolved under controlled perturbation regimes, audited under the same economics (calibration-freeness is purchased, not assumed). That line produced three verdict-grade laws, each stating that selection prices invariance, and each exporting a falsifiable cellular prediction: (1) cheapest sufficient invariance: a pathway's coding regime should exactly match the pricing of its perturbation environment (falsifier: systematic over-insurance; note the P2 adjudication has already ruled out the size-gain operationalisation of this clause, and the clause has contracted accordingly); (2) estimator ordering: sensory adaptors should pay content-noise prices while homeostatic setpoints pay reference-drift prices (falsifier: the two sensitivities do not decouple); (3) two-factor code switching: coding regime should be insensitive to noise severity but switch under noise-type swap at matched severity (falsifier: the cheapest of the three to test, by direct synthetic-biology assay). All three are frozen here in advance of testing; failure adjudicates equally, and if cellular circuits do not obey the cross-domain economics of invariance, the digital-limit principle is a cellular special case.

**An experimental protocol for the unused signature.** The hysteresis signature of the discrimination scheme has never been measured in the p53 literature: all published dose series are one-directional scans. We propose the direct test: scan DNA-damage dose upward and downward in single cells and compare onset and extinction points. The framework predicts zero hysteresis if the core generator is purely excitable; any nonzero width forces reclassification into the subcritical column and downgrades "excluding pure negative feedback" to "excluding supercritical NF". The same experiment adjudicates whether Lemma 1's subcritical escape hatch is realised by nature.

**Boundaries and limitations.** Seven considerations bound the present claims. First, we do not claim analogue information is never used: the blind-adjudication ledger bounds the claim empirically (amplitude stratification falsified for Msn2; duration-axis decoding falsified for NF-κB; free event-time dose decoding falsified for ERK–KTR), and where cells do report graded quantities, morphogen gradients being the classical case, the payment theorem predicts the calibration cost must be paid explicitly (reference points, memory, physical contact), and the known biology of those systems reads as a catalogue of such payments. Second, our information statements concern steady-state and single-readout channels; trajectory-level information can exceed snapshot capacity, and the digital-limit principle bounds what is robustly transferable per unit of calibration effort, not what is transferable in principle. Third, two proofs are representative rather than fully general: Lemma 2 is stated for time-scale-separated systems with fast-subsystem folds, and Lemma 1's Hopf-onset argument is genericity-based, with the subcritical exception handled by additional signatures. Fourth, the chemotaxis rupture is a statement about a model class, not about the bacterium: whether the repair is structural (a missing state variable) or statistical (a different readout model) is an open problem we have registered, with a prospective arm awaiting the necessary dataset. Fifth, the GPCR meta-audit quantifies inconsistency, not misconduct; its deliverable is a map of where quantitative conclusions rest on unaudited parameters, plus a checklist (cell-level identity reconciliation) that any extracted bias factor should pass before use. Sixth, the molecular-layer decomposition of the p53 timing constants carries three registered boundaries (SI S14.5): the linear-eigenvalue period overshoots the simulated period by 8.5% and the simulated pulse width sits below the archived band, both consistent with the mechanism core running slightly fast and narrow under the nominal calibration; the width response to Wip1 perturbation conflicts between two literature sources, which we register rather than resolve; and the first-pulse and interpulse closures are consistency closures, since the model constants involved were themselves anchored on the same measured bands. Seventh, the gain-distribution confinement of the M3R-to-Ca cascade does not generalise: pre-registered cross-system tests on ERK and NF-$\kappa$B pulse trains returned confinement ratios 0.59–2.17 against the frozen line of 3 (SI S17c); amplitude in those systems dies by within-cell pulse-to-pulse stochasticity rather than across-cell gain dispersion, with the carrier-level conclusion (frequency and count codes) unaffected.

**Outlook.** The onset-bifurcation classification of encoding has a thirty-year-old precedent in neuronal excitability (Type I/SNIC versus Type II/Hopf)^40^, with the analogue signature living in the frequency rather than the amplitude observable, suggesting a general duality, encoding signature as a function of (bifurcation type $\times$ observable), unifying molecular and electrical pulse codes. The same law has now been found on both sides of the measurement boundary: assay platforms count when they cannot calibrate, and cells, the original uncalibratable instruments, do the same. A companion study extends the audit to intercellular and neural communication and to artificial networks, where the price of paid amplitude can be measured in decodable bits. The cellular layer, established here, is the anchor: four systems, one currency.

## Methods

### Audit procedure

Every system passed through the same five-step audit (dimensionless master equation; exact degeneracy group; Fisher stratification at experimentally realistic noise; one-sided reporting protocols for plateau directions; orthogonal-resolution identification). All verdicts were pre-registered before scoring; failures, including our own pipeline errors, were double-recorded alongside successes and are archived verbatim. Numerical continuation^41^ used custom continuation validated against analytic normal forms (Hopf points to $\pm 10^{-4}$ in the control parameter; $A^2$-linearity $R^2$ reported per branch). Every number quoted in the main text is transcribed from a frozen verdict card, not from session memory.

### PdPC analysis

The symmetric and asymmetric Goldbeter–Koshland steady states were solved by brentq ($\mathrm{xtol} = 10^{-14}$) and nondimensionalised to $(\xi, \kappa)$ and $(\xi, \kappa_1, \kappa_2)$; degeneracy-group actions (rate rescaling; ($K_m$, $S_T$) co-scaling; mixed) were verified pointwise on a 200-point logarithmic titration grid, with invariance holding to machine precision (max $|\Delta u| = 0$). The Fisher information matrix was computed from analytic response Jacobians at observation noise $\sigma = 0.03$; deep-saturation confidence widths for $\ln \kappa$ by Monte Carlo refitting ($n = 400$ synthetic curves per $\kappa$). Mutual-information optima used log-normal input ensembles ($\sigma_\xi = 0.3$ narrow, 1.5 wide), Gaussian output noise $\sigma = 0.03$, adaptive grids converging to $\pm 0.02$ bits. The positive-feedback switch (fixed-point equation $u = F(\xi(1+g\cdot u))$, critical gain $g^{*} = 0.443$ at $\kappa = 0.05$) was audited identically, with all four group actions verified in both the one-root and three-root regions.

### p53 analysis

Topology classification used a canonical three-variable NF model (analytic equilibrium branch; 6000-point stability scan with the $3 \times 3$ analytic Jacobian; unique destabilisation $s_H = 16.4449$ via complex pair $\pm 0.924i$; limit-cycle amplitudes by solve_ivp at $\mathrm{rtol} = 10^{-9}$, $A^2 \propto (s - s_H)$ at $R^2 = 0.9926$) and a time-scale-separated excitable representative (FitzHugh–Nagumo form; 6.5% amplitude variation over a 4.6-fold drive range), with a subcritical normal-form control (quintic-stabilised; measured hysteresis width 0.275 versus theory 0.25) and a Morris–Lecar Type-II control ($\alpha = -0.258$). The counting law $N \approx (\tau_r/T) \cdot \ln(D_0/D_c)$ was fitted over the sustained-counting window with period self-consistency. The channel census propagated the Fisher information of six pulse statistics ($t_1, T, A, N, w, \mathrm{IPI}$) onto seven dynamical parameters, with three numerical configurations ($M = 400$ central, $M = 4000$ forward, $M = 4000$ central differences) confirming the null-direction ratio $\lambda_7/\lambda_1 \leq 4.7 \times 10^{-19}$ and eigenvector $\sigma$-weight 0.996–0.999. The stochastic population model (200 cells per dose; Euler–Maruyama $dt = 0.001$; dynamical noise $\sigma = 0.10$; lognormal gain dispersion and onset jitter; time unit calibrated by median $\mathrm{IPI} = 5.5$ h) generated the gain-dispersion kill and both dispersion signatures. Experimental placement used source-digitised dose series (Lahav et al.^3^ $\gamma$-irradiation 0.1–10 Gy; Mönke et al.^9^ NCS 25–400 $\mathrm{ng\,ml^{-1}}$; Batchelor et al.^8^ Fig. 1G/H colour-segmentation digitisation with visual axis calibration, bootstrap $\times 20000$: $\alpha_{\mathrm{NCS}} = -0.001 \pm 0.113$, $\alpha_{\mathrm{UV}} = +0.981 \pm 0.114$; duration exponents $-0.21$ / $+0.79$; $\tau_r \approx 7.7$ h).

### Digital-limit analysis

The conditional theorem (two-circuit constructive proof of likelihood cancellation onto partition-counting statistics) and its unconditional generalisation (stratified classification over degeneracy groups; universal functional theorem $T'$; noise-robustness bound Theorem N) are stated and proved in SI S5, with the non-tautology analysis (within-bin amplitude moments; the coefficient of variation; continuous ratio statistics; the boundary against representational measurement theory) and the explicit uniqueness domain (single channel, single readout, memoryless, passive sensing).

### Framework formalism and self-checks

The framework of Results section 1 (layer model, degeneracy group, survival set, three-leg criterion, information-loss identity, three reference classes, predictions P-1/P-2/P-3) is stated with full definitions in SI S1. Its development lineage is part of the audit trail: seven internal bugs and sixteen external-review items across versions v2.0–v4.2 were recorded and repaired, including the two substantive corrections of the final version (the survival-set definition moved from raw output distributions to transmitted-alphabet distributions, without which the set is generically empty; and the layer-boundary rule restored). Framework self-checks were run as simulations before any system audit: a p53 cascade three-leg verification (survival set, symbolic reachability, and bandwidth legs checked jointly, with the information-loss identity verified numerically) and a chemotaxis receptor-layer self-check; both are archived with their scripts.

### Cancer-chain audit (P11–P16)

Every link of the chain carried its own pre-registration card with frozen verdict lines before data access (SI S19). ### Audit discipline

Three disciplines made the audit binding rather than rhetorical. First, pre-registration: decision lines, acceptance criteria and exclusion rules for every adjudication were frozen in writing before the corresponding data were unblinded; scoring scripts execute the frozen tables verbatim. Second, frozen verdict cards: every conclusion in this paper lives on a timestamped card (fixed seeds; bootstrap counts; SHA-256 fingerprints in the project ledger) and is quoted from the card, never from memory; frozen verdicts never move, and corrections take the form of appended errata (e.g., adjudication 47b) rather than edits. Third, double-recording: failures are archived with the same completeness as successes: falsified clauses (six in the present ledger), rejected hypotheses (two in the NF-κB line), and our own pipeline errors (three in the chemotaxis line, one in P5), each with cause and repair. Key adjudications were re-executed independently in a clean cloud environment from the frozen scripts. Version history functions as the audit trail: the one mechanistic-extrapolation failure of the programme (the ERK amplitude prediction) is preserved in the record, with its cause and its consequence for the claim's domain.
