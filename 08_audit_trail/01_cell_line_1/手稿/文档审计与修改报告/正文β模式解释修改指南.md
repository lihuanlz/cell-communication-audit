## ③ SI 同步（建议，S2c.5.2 末尾）

**定位**：Ctrl+F 搜 `S2c.5.3 Why dPCR is structurally unique`，插在它**之前**（(iii) Degeneracy direction 段 `...for all β > 0.` 之后）。SI 正文目前完全没有 Mode A/B 字样，S2c.5.2（Fisher 壁垒）正是双峰后验的理论对应处：

> **Remark (empirical illustration).** Here β is the effective valency (the number of independently binding epitopes per target molecule): Mode A (β ≈ 1) corresponds to monovalent, analog-like binding, and Mode B (β ≳ 30) to the hypervalent regime in which the R2 (β → ∞) limit is effectively exact. A bimodal posterior therefore means that two physically distinct valency regimes fit the data equally well, so that β — and hence Ω = Nβ and M₀ — is not identifiable. The bimodal R1 posterior of the sβG dataset (Mode A: 24%, β ≈ 1; Mode B: 76%, β ≈ 3.7 × 10³; SI Table 3) is the direct experimental manifestation of this barrier: the information geometry above predicts a flat direction, and the posterior resolves it into two separated, near-equivalent regimes.

## ⑤ RIA κ 的可移植性披露（小改，2 处）

**背景**：验证发现 κ 点估计与 bootstrap CI 端点随优化器实现（BLAS/SciPy 版本）漂移——同一脚本同一 seed，作者机器得 κ=697 [1.2, 5.5×10³]、ξ₀ CI 上端 5.45；另一台机器得 κ=1383 [0.1, 5.2×10³]、ξ₀ CI 上端 16.2。这是平脊似然的直接表现。**可移植（跨机器逐位一致）的只有**：点估计 ξ₀/ξ*/R²/RMSE/DW、整个 κ 扫描表（4_Kappa_Scan，6 位小数逐位一致）、扫描下界 κ≥1。脚本已同步改为扫描口径打印下界（`SI_Table_2_RIA_Yalow1960.py`）。

**改法 1**：Methods「Reporting of κ」段，Ctrl+F 搜 `2.1 vs. 1`：

> …(consistent with the scan-based bound, **2.1** vs. 1)

改为（与 n=2000 bootstrap 下界一致）：

> …(consistent with the scan-based bound, **1.2** vs. 1)

**改法 2**：同段 `the bootstrap CI [1.2, 5.5 × 10³] is reported separately as a sampling-based cross-check` 之后插入一句：

> Because the κ direction is flat, the point estimate of κ and the bootstrap CI endpoints vary between optimizer implementations even at a fixed bootstrap seed; the run-invariant statement is the scan-based bound κ ≥ 1 (SI Table 2).
