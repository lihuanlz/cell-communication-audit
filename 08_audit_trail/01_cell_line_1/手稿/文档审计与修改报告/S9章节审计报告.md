### S9-E【小】S9.4 末句 "in the intermediate-$\kappa$ regime" 与本节推导层级不符

**锚点**：搜 `geometric consequence of the TCS kinetic equation`。

**改法**：改为 "The linearity of $t/q_t$ vs $t$, widely used for PSO fitting, is an exact geometric consequence of the ($n=2$, irreversible, $\kappa\to\infty$) corner of the TCS kinetic equation — and remains approximately valid through the intermediate-$\kappa$ regime — not evidence of a bimolecular surface reaction¹¹."
