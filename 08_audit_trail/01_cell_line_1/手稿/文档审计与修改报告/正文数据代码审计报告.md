## 四、两个悬案的代码确认

### ① κ₁ ≈ κ₂（Fig. 2a）——答案：HDI 大幅重叠，用"重叠版"措辞
κ₁ = 0.1434 **[0.1003, 0.1995]** 与 κ₂ = 0.1686 **[0.1100, 0.2568]** 的 95% HDI 明显重叠（重叠区 [0.11, 0.20]），且代码中**没有包被饱和模型**。结论：采用审计报告建议的情况 B 措辞——

> The 95% HDIs of κ₁ and κ₂ overlap substantially; the decade-spanning contrast is carried by κ₃, as expected for a surface density approaching the unsaturated coating regime.

### ② 标本计数——答案：Methods 的 "26 matched" 是错的，n=27–29 是对的
代码证据（`simoa fit 2023 iL6`）：**全部 34 个标本（26 plasma + 8 in-house）都有 Roche 参考值**（S-1 至 S-34）；成对比较排除 Roche=0（空白）与模型 NaN 后，4PL 得 n=29、R2 得 n=27——正文 "n = 27–29 valid pairs" **正确** ✅。错在 Methods 那句 "26 plasma specimens have matched Roche values"（把"26 个血浆标本"误写成了"26 个有 Roche 匹配"）。
**改法**：Methods 该句改为——

> The test set comprised 34 specimens (26 plasma, 8 in-house prepared), all with Roche Cobas reference values; blank (Roche = 0) and out-of-range results were excluded pairwise, yielding n = 27–29 valid pairs for the Roche comparison depending on the back-calculation model, and n = 30 specimens with valid Simoa back-calculations (4 excluded above the ULOQ).
